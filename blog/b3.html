June 6, 2026 Letter To Grandma

Dear Grandma,
I have spent some time reflecting on my life trying to make sense of its purpose. My idea of what my purpose is has changed significantly in recent years. What I understood is that my purpose came form my ability to make an impact. Knowing this and knowing my life and influence to be quite small I began to wonder how I would make any sort of impact. I reflected on those that had made an impact on me.

May this serve as a gentle reminder that some messages are better written slowly, using thoughtful words that linger beautifully on the page. We invite you to embrace this classic aesthetic and share the art of timeless communication with others.

Your Best Girl
